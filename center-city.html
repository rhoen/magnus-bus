About these departures

Live locations show the reported next stop and stops remaining to your boarding stop. The route diagram follows the closest vehicle. Canceled trips and vehicles already past your stop are excluded.

Trolley locations can be unavailable underground. When no fresh location is available, we show departure times from SEPTA’s trip updates: “Live estimate” means SEPTA marks the trip as real time; “Scheduled” means the timetable only. These times are not a guarantee. No position is invented for a trolley without location data.

The page refreshes every 15 seconds while visible. Location reports over two minutes old and departure results checked over 45 seconds ago are hidden. If one route fails, the other routes can still update.
