About this stop

This sticker selects a fixed boarding stop, route, and travel direction. Check the stop name and direction above before boarding. You don’t need to share your location.

The cards show how many stops remain from each bus’s reported next stop to yours, following its actual trip. “Your stop is next” means your boarding stop is the next reported stop, not a guarantee that the bus is already here. Traffic and detours can change arrival order.

Updates refresh every 15 seconds while this page is visible. Locations older than two minutes are hidden. No bus in view does not mean service has stopped. For trolleys without a fresh position, departure times may appear instead, labeled Live estimate or Scheduled.
